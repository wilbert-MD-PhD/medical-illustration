# What happens under an adhesive bandage?

[中文](README.md) · [Full comic](comic-v1.4.jpg) · [PDF](review-v1.4.pdf) · [Chinese script](script.md)

Xiaoman cuts her finger while folding a paper airplane. The story moves beneath the dressing to show clotting, debris clearance and epithelial coverage. Six panels, original characters, production revision v1.4.

![Comic](comic-v1.4.jpg)

## From downloaded references to artwork

The user supplied the comic brief, not these three reference images. Following the medical-illustration Skill and project rules, Codex located the original pages, downloaded figures from **Frontiers** and **NIH/NIAID BioArt**, opened the local images, checked captions and permitted uses, and attached the files to the image-generation tool. Suitable existing references were reused.

- **R05 — Nike et al. (2022), Fig. 1:** skin layers and wound-edge epithelial coverage, informing panels 3 and 5. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbioe.2022.865014), CC BY 4.0.
- **R11 — Wang et al. (2022), Fig. 1:** keratinocyte shapes and the epidermis/dermis relationship. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2022.789274), CC BY 4.0. The reference's hair follicle and full immune-cell inventory were not copied into a fingertip story.
- **R02 — Ryan Kissinger / NIAID Visual & Medical Arts, Macrophage:** cell body, extensions and nucleus for panel 4. [Source](https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/bioart/309), Public Domain. Engulfment action was cross-checked separately.

All three were actual attachments in the first successful generation, G02, alongside the NIH skin cross-section and platelet/fibrin microscopy. The initial seven-image attempt, G01, was rejected before generation. Later revisions preserved the source chain; the final G10 edit used earlier comic artwork and the NIH skin reference, rather than downloading or attaching every reference again.

WebP files were converted to PNG where needed without changing image content. These references are professional medical illustrations, not patient photographs. The comic reinterprets the relationships with new characters, composition and colors. See the [reference gallery](README.md), [full credits and licenses](CREDITS.md), [download URLs and hashes](sources.json), and [actual successful prompt and attachment order](generation-record.json).

## What was verified

Reference retrieval, image viewing, reference-conditioned generation and revision, and editable Illustrator lettering were performed. The native AI was reopened: 22 text frames, 16 translucent rounded boxes and six speech tails. The final PDF was rendered and visually inspected. Native production files and full machine-specific logs remain in the production project.

This demonstrates one production run with user feedback, not success rates across models or a fresh-install benchmark. The separately shipped generic Illustrator helper has its own validation status; see [validation](../../validation.md). Human medical sign-off remains pending.
